An RFC is a pull request that adds `docs/rfc/<date>-<slug>.md` from this template, labelled `type:rfc`. The whole cycle takes one to two weeks. An accepted RFC ends as an ADR; a rejected or withdrawn one stays as the record of why.

## Problem and classification
What problem, for whom, with what evidence? Systemic (several teams, services or repositories, or hard to reverse) or local? Decide it in 15 minutes: a local decision is an ADR, not an RFC.

## Options
At least two, including the simplest viable one and doing nothing; benefits, costs and risks of each.

## Recommendation
The option proposed, why, and what it costs to reverse.

## Timebox
Discussion opens and closes on the dates above (5 days); the decision is taken on the date above, in a 30-minute meeting or in writing, even if some questions stay open.

## Decision
Accepted, rejected or withdrawn, by whom, on what date, and the link to the ADR that records it.
